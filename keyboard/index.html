Connect your keyboard in 3 steps

  1. Over USB

    USB-C iPad: a direct USB-C cable. Lightning iPad: Apple's Lightning to USB 3 Camera Adapter, with its charging port.

  2. Over Bluetooth

    Turn the keyboard on, then pair it from the app's MIDI screen, not from Settings › Bluetooth.

  3. Play

    Start a challenge: the app recognises every note you play. On Android, USB is more reliable than Bluetooth.
